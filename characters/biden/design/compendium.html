Core mechanics

Drowsy: sleepiness is on him

  • Doze X: gain X Drowsy. Sleepy Joe also Dozes 2 at the end of each of his turns.
  • When Drowsy reaches his nod-off line (10), he nods off: his turn ends right away.
  • Some cards say Nod off: strong effects that put him to sleep on purpose.
  • Crossing the line at the end of a turn costs nothing; crossing it early wastes the rest of the turn.

The meter is a timer you manage: a bigger nap means bigger lasers, but play the sleepy cards last.

Dark Brandon: the clarity

  • He wakes up at the start of the turn after he nods off, or right away with a Wake Up card.
  • Laser Eyes fire when he wakes: damage equal to his Drowsy to ALL enemies, then Drowsy resets.
  • For that turn: Tangents do all their lines, Drowsy can't build, and "As Dark Brandon" bonuses apply.

Wake Up early for a small laser now, or sleep on it for a big one next turn.

Tangent: confusion is on him

  • A Tangent card has 2 or 3 lines, and only the lit one happens. Line 1 is lit when you draw it.
  • After you play any other card, each Tangent in your hand moves to its next line.
  • As Dark Brandon, a Tangent does every line.
Here's the Deal, after you play a Strike:

2Gain 7 Block.
3Draw 2 cards.

Every turn is a little puzzle about order, or you save the card for Dark Brandon and get all three.

Play styles: each one works on its own

How the styles connect (optional)

Bridges

  • Nap and Dark Brandon: every nap ends in a Dark Brandon turn. Nap decks bank big lasers; Brandon decks cash in small and often.
  • Tangents and Dark Brandon: awake turns make every Tangent do everything; the Teleprompter makes them free that turn.
  • Nap and Tangents: Sound Asleep keeps the hand, so Tangents keep turning while he sleeps on it.

Tensions

  1. Nap now or push closer? More Drowsy is a bigger laser, but crossing mid-turn ends the turn.
  2. Wake Up now or sleep on it? Wake Up gives Dark Brandon this turn, with whatever Drowsy you have.
  3. Which line? Plan the order of the turn, or hold the Tangent for Dark Brandon.

  • Tangent turn: Off the Cuff is on line 1 (10 damage). Two cards later it's on line 3: 3 damage to ALL enemies 3 times, the right call against four Inklets.

Tone

The jokes land on the persona: dozing off, "resting my eyes", losing the thread, rambling anecdotes, "Here's the deal", "C'mon, man!", "Malarkey!", the aviators, chocolate chip ice cream, Amtrak, the '67 Corvette and the Dark Brandon meme. Sleepiness and confusion are slapstick: pajamas, snores, speech bubbles wandering off.

Off limits: age, decline or health; real people other than him; immigrants or any real ethnic or religious group; real tragedies; anything mean toward real victims. Enemies are always Spire monsters.
