Core mechanics

The Wall

  • Build X raises your Wall's height by X.
  • The Wall doesn't soak damage. Height only drops when you spend it (Demolition, Wrecking Ball).
  • Every 10 height is a Section, with no limit. Each Section gives 3 Block at the end of your turn.
  • Reaching a height milestone unlocks a stage perk that lasts the whole fight, even after a Demolition.

Build toward the next stage, feel the jump when it lands, then decide whether to keep building or cash in. Not Osty: it never takes hits, never dies, never attacks on its own.

Deport

  • If the enemy is at or below your Deport line (25% of its max HP), it leaves combat.
  • Raise the line with Border Control (+10%, stacks), Border Wall (+2% per Section) and Mass Deportation (doubled).
  • Deported enemies drop no Gold. Bosses are immune; elites and minions are not.
  • Not a kill: no on-death effects and not Fatal. HP bars show the line; a DENIED stamp appears under it.

Works in any deck that deals damage, no Wall needed. The question is always: kill for gold, or Deport for tempo?

Tariff

  • Enemy debuff: whenever it attacks, you gain X Gold. Stacks, lasts all combat.

"They'll pay for it." Enemy aggression becomes income.

Pay X Gold

  • Playable only with at least X Gold; lose X Gold. Glows red when you can't afford it.

Gold becomes a second combat resource that competes with the shop.

Tweet

  • Token card: 0 cost, deal 3 (5) damage to ALL enemies, Exhaust.

Chip damage everywhere, and free cards to play.

Play styles: each one works on its own

How the styles connect (optional)

Bridges

  • Wall Street, Gold-Plated Bricks and They'll Pay For It turn the Wall into Gold; Hire Contractors turns Gold into Wall.
  • Border Wall raises the Deport line per Section.
  • Ratings turns Tweets into Wall; Tweets chip everything under the line for Law and Order.

Tensions

  1. Keep building or cash in? Demolition spends height, but reached stages stay unlocked.
  2. Kill or Deport? Deport is faster but gives no Gold; Hostile Takeover and Cornerstone only pay off on a kill.
  3. Shop or combat? Pay-Gold cards are strong for their cost because they drain your wallet.

Example turns

  • Wall deck, turn 1: 6 height from the shovel, Build the Wall to 11 (fence, 1 Section = 3 Block a turn), Groundbreaking to 14 and draw a card. By turn 3 Pour Concrete passes 25: Brick stage, an extra card every turn from then on.
  • Wall deck, boss fight: Infrastructure Week adds 7 a turn. At 70 the Big Beautiful Wall hits every enemy for 7 each turn, Guard Towers add 14 more, and Demolition deals 70 at once; the stages stay after tearing it down.
  • Deport deck, four 11 HP Inklets: the line is at 2.75 HP, 3.85 with Border Control. ALL CAPS drops them to 5, a Tweet to 2: Law and Order Deports all four at end of turn.
  • Deals turn at 220 Gold: Bribe (−20 Gold, +2 Energy), Net Worth for 25 damage, then Slap a Tariff on the big hitter so it pays for every swing.

Tone

The jokes land on the persona: the bombast ("tremendous", "bigly", "sad!"), gold on everything, deal-making, all-caps posting, golf, two scoops, the long red tie, Infrastructure Week. Walls and deportation are game mechanics aimed at Spire monsters, shown as slapstick: security guards, EXIT turnstiles, gold buses.

Off limits: real people other than him, jokes about immigrants or any real ethnic or religious group, real tragedies, and anything mean toward real victims.
